KEY FINDINGS:
----------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Genome Length Effect:
   As genome length increases, the Lander-Waterman equation predicts
   very few contigs at 10x coverage. Our results match this prediction.

2. Coverage Effect:
   Higher coverage leads to fewer contigs and better N50 values.
   The Lander-Waterman predictions are accurate at all coverage levels.

3. Error Rate Effect:
   Sequencing errors create false branch points in the De Bruijn graph,
   leading to more contigs than predicted. The Lander-Waterman equation
   does NOT account for errors, so accuracy decreases with error rate.
