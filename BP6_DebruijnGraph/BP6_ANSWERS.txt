PART 3: N50 OF THE ASSEMBLY
================================================================================

N50 = 21 letters

Calculation Method:
The N50 is defined as the contig length such that 50% of the total assembly
is contained in contigs of that length or longer.

Calculation Steps:
1. Sort contigs by length (descending): 49, 48, 21, 21, 17, 17, 13, 13, 13...
2. Total assembly length = 275 letters
3. Cumulative sum until reaching 275 / 2 = 137.5 letters:
   - 49 letters (cumsum = 49)
   - 48 letters (cumsum = 97)
   - 21 letters (cumsum = 118)
   - 21 letters (cumsum = 139) ← crosses 50% threshold

4. N50 = 21 letters

Interpretation:
- Half of the assembled sequence is in contigs of length 21 or greater
- This indicates moderate assembly quality
- The presence of two long contigs (49 and 48 letters) represents successful
  assembly of the major portions of the quote
- The remaining assembly is fragmented into smaller contigs due to branch
  points in the graph

ADDITIONAL ANALYSIS
================================================================================

K-mer Statistics:
- Total unique 5-mers: 207
- Total k-mer observations: 1,456
- Average coverage: 7.03x
- Most frequent k-mer: "shall" (count = 18)
- Least frequent k-mer: "again" (count = 1)

Why Multiple Contigs?
Despite being a single continuous quote, the assembly broke into 17 contigs
because:

1. Repeated Words: Common words like "all", "that", "shall" appear multiple
   times, creating branch points where the assembler must choose between
   multiple valid paths.

2. Graph Structure: Each branch point forces the creation of a new contig
   because the assembler cannot determine which path is correct without
   additional information.

3. Conservative Assembly: The algorithm stops at branch points rather than
   making potentially incorrect choices, ensuring accuracy over completeness.

Examples of Repeated Sequences:
- "shall" appears in multiple contexts (3 locations in poem)
- "that" appears 4 times
- "all" appears 3 times
- These repetitions create the 14 branch/merge points in the graph

Assembly Quality:
The N50 of 21 letters with 17 contigs represents a reasonable assembly given:
- 7X coverage is moderate (higher coverage would help resolve ambiguities)
- 5-mer k-mer size is quite short (longer k-mers might span repeat regions)
- No paired-end information to help resolve branches
- No errors in the data (realistic sequencing would have errors)

In real genome assembly, this level of fragmentation would be acceptable for
a draft assembly but would benefit from:
- Scaffolding with longer reads
- Gap filling strategies
- Manual curation of obvious overlaps
